This May, Amazon Prime Gaming is offering an exciting lineup of 'free' games, including the highly acclaimed Mafia II: Definitive Edition. As part of an Amazon Prime subscription, gamers can claim, download, and keep these titles, making it a fantastic opportunity for both casual and dedicated players alike.

The transition from Prime Gaming to Amazon Luna has not diminished the value of the service. Players can still enjoy some of last months offerings, such as Tiny Tinas Assault on Dragon Keep and XCOM: Enemy Unknown Complete Pack, ensuring a rich library of games to explore. With the addition of GameNight, a selection of party games designed for casual play, Amazon is clearly aiming to cater to a wide range of gaming preferences.
