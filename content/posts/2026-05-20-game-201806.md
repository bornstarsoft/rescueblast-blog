Harry Krueger, the visionary behind the acclaimed PS5 exclusive Returnal, has embarked on an exciting new journey by founding Cosmic Division, a game studio based in Finland. After nearly 15 years with Housemarque, Krueger aims to carve out a unique identity for his new studio, leveraging his extensive experience in crafting award-winning action games. His commitment to creating games that resonate emotionally with players is evident in the studio's mission to deliver a gameplay-first experience.

With Cosmic Division's first project set to be a single-player IP for PC and consoles, the studio promises to prioritize gameplay while weaving in a compelling narrative. This fresh venture not only reflects Krueger's dedication to innovation but also highlights the growing trend of experienced developers venturing out to create their own studios. As Cosmic Division seeks collaborators and talent, it will be interesting to see how they push the boundaries of interactive storytelling and gameplay.

As we look to the future, one can't help but wonder: what new heights will Cosmic Division reach in the ever-evolving landscape of gaming? Will they redefine the player experience once again?
